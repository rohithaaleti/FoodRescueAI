# FoodRescue AI

## Project Vision

FoodRescue AI is an intelligent food redistribution platform that connects restaurants, hotels, hostels, NGOs, volunteers, and community refrigerators to reduce food waste and ensure surplus food reaches people before it expires.

- Restaurant
- NGO
- Volunteer
- Admin
